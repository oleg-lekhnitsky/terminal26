# Generated weather backgrounds

Created with the built-in image_gen tool and applied in WeatherCard.vue. Assets live beside this document. Condition mapping: app/utils/weatherSky.ts. Rain, snow, fog, overcast and storm take priority over dawn/dusk; sunrise/sunset appear within 45 minutes of the provider's local sun times in clear or partly cloudy conditions. Condition images receive a 50% brightness treatment at night. Existing day/night sky studies are retained for other clear/partly-cloudy periods. Images are decoded before Flow crossfades.

Source for daily sun times: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs
