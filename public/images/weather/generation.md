# Weather background studies

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Applied in WeatherCard.vue according to the provider's local day/night flag, with condition-based palette tints and a readability scrim. These two studies provide atmospheric day/night backgrounds, not separate illustrations of rain, snow, or storms.

## sky-day-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait 4:5 weather-widget background, full bleed sky only. Create an elegant Apple Weather-inspired daytime partly cloudy sky: luminous natural blue atmospheric gradient, subtly volumetric soft white cloud banks mostly along the lower edge and outer corners, gentle diffused sunlight, physically believable airy depth, polished understated mobile weather aesthetic. Keep central 60% and top-center calm, blue and low-detail so large white typography can be overlaid. No text, numbers, icons, UI, logos, frames, borders, horizon, landscape or objects. Not a screenshot: only the reusable sky background. Fine smooth gradients, no banding, no dramatic sun disc.

## sky-night-v1.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait 4:5 weather-widget background, full bleed sky only. Create an elegant Apple Weather-inspired nighttime partly cloudy sky: deep navy to muted indigo atmospheric gradient, subtle moonlight illuminating soft cloud banks near the bottom and outer edges, a few tiny restrained stars, polished realistic volumetric atmosphere. Keep central 60% and top-center dark, calm and low-detail for white temperature typography. No visible moon disc. No text, numbers, icons, UI, logos, frames, borders, horizon, landscape or objects. Only a reusable night sky background, not a screenshot. Smooth gradients, restrained contrast, no fantasy nebula, no oversaturated purple.
